\begin{column}{\colwidth}

  \begin{block}{\blocktitleformat{What is VeriFi}}
    A finance social media app where users publish structured market claims and suggested positions that are signed, verifiable, and resolved against real data.

    \vspace{0.7cm}
    \subtitleformat{Core proposition.}\\
    A user's financial reputation on VeriFi cannot be faked or retroactively edited.
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{\blocktitleformat{Motivation}}
    Existing platforms give investors lots of opinions, but weak accountability:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item posts can be deleted or reworded after the outcome,
      \item expertise is self-declared instead of measured,
      \item screenshots and social proof are easy to manipulate,
      \item vague predictions cannot be resolved objectively.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{0.7cm}
    VeriFi supplies all three missing properties: \textbf{social}, \textbf{verifiable}, and \textbf{quantifiable}.

    \vspace{0.8cm}
    \begin{table}
      \centering
      \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.13}

  \begin{block}{\blocktitleformat{Components}}
    \textbf{Claims, Positions, Reputation Score, Reputation Market, Channels, Proofs}

    \vspace{0.8cm}
    \subtitleformat{Hard Claims:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item asset, direction, percentage, deadline
      \item signed by the user's wallet
      \item resolved after expiry using market data
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{\blocktitleformat{Design}}
    \subtitleformat{All content is signed by users.}\\
    VeriFi uses wallet-based identity. Users authenticate with a native browser wallet or MetaMask, and the private key stays on the client.

    \vspace{0.8cm}
    \subtitleformat{Proofs make claims portable.}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item claim payload,
      \item wallet address,
      \item signature,
      \item timestamp.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{0.7cm}
    \subtitleformat{Future work.}\\
    Train a VeriFi-owned finance LLM for the LLM extractor component, so claim parsing becomes more domain-specific and less dependent on generic extraction.

  \begin{block}{\blocktitleformat{How the Reputation Market Works}}
    \textbf{Reputation Market ensures:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item early foresighters are rewarded more,
      \item copycat predictions do not dominate,
      \item trivial claims are not rewarded heavily,
      \item wrong claims reduce credibility.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{0.7cm}
    \textbf{What VeriFi brings:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item measurable finance discussion,
      \item public profiles backed by resolved claims,
      \item proof files that anyone can verify,
      \item channels for focused communities,
      \item a reputation layer based on outcomes.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}
